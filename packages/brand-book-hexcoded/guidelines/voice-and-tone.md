# Voice & Tone · Hexcoded

> **L10 lock**: Confident-direct register. No marketing fluff.
> **L11 lock**: Hexcoded is a verb.

## The voice

Hexcoded sounds **direct-minimal**. Short sentences. Concrete verbs. No fluff. Mid-sentence pause for clarity, not for poetry.

The default speaker is calmly confident — the kind of person who knows the answer and gets to the point. We don't pad. We don't apologise for ourselves. We don't announce that we're "thrilled" or "delighted".

## Four registers

We carry the same voice across four registers; the dial shifts by surface.

### A · Direct minimal (default)

Product UI · errors · system messages.

> "Couldn't save. Try again."

Five words. No apology theatre. Diagnosis + next step.

### B · Confident marketing

Landing · pricing · launch. Still concrete, but takes a stance.

> "Take your brief. Hexcode it. Ship in minutes, not weeks."

Three short statements. Action verbs. Specific outcome.

### C · Helpful guide

Onboarding · empty states · docs.

> "Drop a brief here. We'll turn it into a draft you can edit in seconds."

Speaks to a real action. Sets a clear expectation. Uses "we" sparingly.

### D · Quiet authority

Pricing · contracts · legal · security.

> "Your content stays yours. We don't train on it. Ever."

Three sentences. The third one is the contract.

## Sentence shape

- Open with the subject + verb. Not the qualifier.
- One idea per sentence.
- Cut ramp-up words: "It's important to note that…", "We're excited to announce…", "What if you could…"
- Verbs > nouns. "Hexcode your brief" > "the hexcoding of your brief".
- Hexcoded as a verb when it reads naturally. UPPERCASE only as the brand mark.
