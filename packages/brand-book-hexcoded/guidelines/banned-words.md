# Banned words · Hexcoded · L12 lock

12 words that never appear in any Hexcoded surface — UI, marketing, support, social, sales decks, contracts.

These kill the brand's confident-direct voice on contact.

| # | Banned | Why · what to say instead |
|---|--------|---------------------------|
| 1 | **synergy** | Vague consultancy filler. Say "what we do together" or describe the actual joint outcome. |
| 2 | **leverage** | Replace with "use". |
| 3 | **ecosystem** | Replace with "platform", "tools we work with", or the actual list. |
| 4 | **revolutionize** | Empty claim. Describe the actual change. |
| 5 | **game-changing** | Empty claim. Describe the actual impact. |
| 6 | **disrupt** | Industry-speak. Say what we actually replace or fix. |
| 7 | **cutting-edge** | Empty claim. Name the actual capability. |
| 8 | **solution** | Generic noun. Say "tool", "service", "platform", or the actual product name. |
| 9 | **empower** | Condescending. Show, don't claim. |
| 10 | **seamless** | Overused. Show how friction is removed. |
| 11 | **world-class** | Empty claim. Show with proof (testimonials, numbers, demos). |
| 12 | **next-generation** | Tech-bro filler. Describe what's new. |

## Enforcement

- Marketing CMS validates copy against this list before publish.
- Sales / support templates ship with the list pinned to the top of the editor.
- CI lint on long-form docs flags any of these words in PRs.
