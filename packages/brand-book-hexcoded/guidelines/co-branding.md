# Co-branding · Hexcoded · L23 lock

Three rules govern any side-by-side appearance of `HEXCODED` with another brand mark.

## 1 · Equal-height wordmarks

Both marks render at identical x-height. Not equal pixel-height of the bounding box — equal **optical** height of the visible letterforms.

## 2 · Vertical separator

A 1px vertical hairline at 50% opacity between the two marks. No icon, no slash, no "+". The hairline reads as a parity divider, not a hierarchy.

## 3 · 1× cap-height clear space

Equal whitespace on both sides of the separator, sized to one cap-height of the wordmark. Never less. Adjust the entire lockup smaller if needed to preserve this.

## Sample lockup

```
HEXCODED  │  PARTNER
        ↕  ↕
        1× cap-height each side
```

## Don'ts

- Don't tilt either wordmark.
- Don't place Hexcoded second when we are the primary surface owner.
- Don't recolour the partner mark to match Tech Green.
- Don't use co-branding lockups for endorsements where Hexcoded is incidental — use the plain Hexcoded mark with the endorsement copy below it instead.
