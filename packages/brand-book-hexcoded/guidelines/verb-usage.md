# Verb usage · Hexcoded · L11 lock

**Hexcoded is a verb.** This is brand IP. Like "Google it" or "Photoshop it" — we own a verb in the cultural conversation.

## Conjugations

- **Present**: hexcode → "Hexcode your brief."
- **Past**: hexcoded → "Hexcoded by 2,400+ teams."
- **Gerund**: hexcoding → "Hexcoding takes about 90 seconds."

## Casing rule

- **Always lowercase** when used as a verb: hexcode, hexcoded, hexcoding.
- **UPPERCASE** only as the brand mark: `HEXCODED`.

## When to use the verb (and when not to)

Aim for ~50% verb use across marketing copy, falling to ~30% in product UI where direct functional language wins.

Use the verb when:

- It reads naturally and short.
- The sentence is action-oriented.
- The reader benefits from the brand association ("Hexcode it" — they remember the brand by doing the thing).

Don't force it when:

- The sentence reads better with "use Hexcoded" or just "Hexcoded".
- A direct functional verb is clearer ("generate", "edit", "approve").
- The audience needs disambiguation (legal, contracts, B2B sales decks early in the relationship).
