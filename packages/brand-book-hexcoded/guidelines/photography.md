# Photography · Hexcoded · L20 lock

> **Brand photos: sparingly · Product imagery: extensively** — Hexcoded is a content platform, the generated content is the canvas.

## Use brand photography sparingly

- Case studies (real customers, real teams)
- Careers / team pages
- Founder / leadership press kits

Avoid stock photos. Avoid hands-holding-phones. Avoid generic-marketing humans pointing at things.

## Use product imagery extensively

Hexcoded ships generated content. Show that content. Often.

- Landing hero: a real generated reel from a real pilot brand.
- Pricing page: the kinds of outputs you get per tier.
- Customer stories: side-by-side before/after.
- Social posts: clips of the workflow + the generated output.

The generated content is more interesting than us. Lean into that.

## Rules when brand photos are used

- Natural light. No studio-flash glamour.
- Tight crops on the subject. No environment-as-background filler.
- People doing actual work, not posing.
- One subject per frame. Avoid composite/crowd shots.
- Tech Green appears only as the brand element (mark on apparel, screen content) — never as a colour wash over the photo.

## Treatment

- 4:5 portrait for case-study heros.
- 16:9 for video thumbnails.
- 1:1 for social.
- 5% subtle vignette acceptable. No filters. No grain.
