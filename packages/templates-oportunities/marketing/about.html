Why Oportunities exists.

The Indian creator economy is ~$1.2B and growing 25% YoY — but brand-creator matching still happens over WhatsApp DMs and shared spreadsheets. We're fixing that, one intent signal at a time.

The problem we're solving.

Brand teams in India spend 60–80% of their time on creator discovery and outreach — and most of it is reactive. By the time you spot Komal Pandey in Bali, three competitors have already pitched her. By the time you brief for Lakmé Fashion Week, the calendar is full.

Creators leak intent constantly — through Stories, trip captions, podcast appearances, product Q&As. The signal is already in the open. What's missing is a system that listens.

"Heads up — we want to make brand-creator matching feel less like cold outreach, and more like reading the room."

Who we serve.

Today: D2C brand teams running creator partnerships at scale — Mamaearth, Mokobara, boAt, Plum, INDmoney, Cred. Soon: brand houses, performance agencies, and the in-house teams at fashion week sponsors and quick-commerce players.

We're an Amplify product. Made in Bengaluru. Indian-creator-economy first.
