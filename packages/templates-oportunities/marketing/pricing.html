Questions, answered.

What counts as a "signal"?

Any actionable creator intent — a planned trip, a product drop, a collab inquiry, a life moment that matches your brief. One creator can produce multiple signals.

Can I switch tiers mid-cycle?

Yes. Upgrades pro-rate immediately. Downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle so you don't lose paid signal capacity.

Do you support GST invoicing?

Yes — all invoices include GSTIN, HSN code, and your registered business address. You can update GST details from Settings → Billing.

What payment methods do you accept?

UPI, all major credit/debit cards, NetBanking, and corporate billing for Growth+ tiers. Indian Rupee only.

Is there a free trial of Pro?

Yes — first 14 days are full Pro access, no card required. Heads up: you can downgrade to Free anytime without losing your saved signals.

How is creator data sourced?

Public social signals, RSS, podcast feeds, and consented partner data. We never scrape DMs or buy contact lists. Every creator has a "remove me" path.
