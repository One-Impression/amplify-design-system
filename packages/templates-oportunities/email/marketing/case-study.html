Before Oportunities, Mamaearth's partnerships team ran on three WhatsApp groups, two Notion docs, and one weekly spreadsheet that nobody trusted. Discovery was reactive — they'd spot a creator after she posted, then race two other brands to her DMs.

In month one with Oportunities, they switched to intent-first: surface the signal early, send the AI-drafted brief while it's warm, track replies in one inbox. The result was 22 booked partnerships in 30 days — more than the previous quarter combined.

"We stopped chasing posts and started catching intent. That changed everything."
— Ghazal Sengupta, Brand Partnerships Lead, Mamaearth
